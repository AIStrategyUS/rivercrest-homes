The Points Where a Traditional Sale Usually Breaks.

Buyer financing gets denied after inspection. The appraisal comes in low and the deal gets renegotiated or dropped. A contingency deadline is missed. None of these exist when there's no buyer mortgage in the transaction.

When You Should Probably List Instead.

If your house is already move-in ready, you're not in a hurry, and your neighborhood is selling fast, a good agent may net you more over a few months. We'll tell you that on the phone if it's true. We'd rather you trust us on the properties where a fast cash sale is genuinely the better math.
