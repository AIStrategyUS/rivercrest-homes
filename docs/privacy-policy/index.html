Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

Who We Are

This website, tnhomecashoffer.com (the “Site”), is owned and operated by Rivercrest Homes LLC (“Rivercrest,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a real estate investment company that purchases residential properties directly from homeowners in Middle Tennessee, including the Nashville and Clarksville areas.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Site or communicating with us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

When you fill out a form on our Site, call or text us, respond to our outreach, or speak with us in person, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • The address and details of the property you may be interested in selling
  • Information about your situation and selling goals that you choose to share
  • The date, time, and content of your communications with us

Information from other sources

Because part of our business involves reaching out to property owners directly, we may also obtain information about properties and their owners from sources such as:

  • Public records, including county assessor, register of deeds, and court records
  • Licensed data providers and marketing lists
  • Referrals and other publicly available sources

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we use cookies, web beacons, pixels, and similar technologies to understand how visitors use the Site and to deliver a better experience. Our systems and service providers may automatically log information such as your browser type, operating system, device identifiers, IP address, the pages you view, and the things you click on or search for. This Site uses advertising and analytics tools, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and Google advertising services.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Evaluate properties and prepare cash offers
  • Respond to your inquiries and communicate with you by phone, text message, email, mail, or in person
  • Contact property owners who may be interested in selling
  • Complete real estate transactions you choose to enter into with us
  • Operate, improve, and secure the Site
  • Market our services, including through search engine marketing and pay-per-click advertising
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

Phone calls with us may be monitored or recorded for quality assurance and record-keeping purposes.

Text Messaging (SMS/MMS)

If you provide your mobile number to us, whether through the Site, over the phone, or in person, you may receive SMS or MMS messages from us about your property, your inquiry, our offers, and related updates. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or sale.

  • Message frequency varies; you may receive up to 4 messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You may receive one final message confirming your opt-out.
  • For help, reply HELP to any message or contact us at info@tnhomecashoffer.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share personal information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers and vendors. Companies that perform services on our behalf, such as website hosting, messaging platforms, advertising, analytics, and data services. Their use of your information is limited to providing those services and is subject to confidentiality obligations.
  • Transaction parties. If you move forward with a sale, we may share information with title companies, escrow agents, attorneys, lenders, and other parties necessary to complete the transaction.
  • Legal and safety. When we have a good-faith belief that disclosure is required by law, or is necessary to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.
  • Business transfers. To a successor or purchaser in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets. We will attempt to notify you of such a transfer to the extent permitted by law.
  • With your consent. Any other sharing with your express permission.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to safeguard the information we hold, and our vendors provide assurances that they do the same for data held on our behalf. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and data security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Choices & Rights

  • Text messages: reply STOP at any time to opt out.
  • Phone calls: tell us you no longer wish to be contacted, and we will add you to our internal do-not-contact list.
  • Emails: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, or contact us directly.
  • Access & deletion: you may contact us at any time to request to see, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you.

Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites or services. This policy does not apply to those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
