Process Transparency

Here's Exactly What Happens, Step by Step.

No call center, no algorithm, no guesswork. This is the same process we walk every homeowner through, in the order it actually happens.

1

You Reach Out

A quick call, text, or form submission with your name, phone number, and the property address. No commitment, no cost.

2

We Talk Through Your Situation

We follow up by phone or text to hear about your situation and the condition of the home, and ask for a few current interior and exterior photos so we understand what we're working with.

3

We Run the Numbers

Once we have what we need, we work up a fair, as-is cash offer for your property. Depending on the situation, we may also schedule an in-person walkthrough before finalizing a number.

4

You Review the Offer, No Pressure

If it works for you, we send over a straightforward purchase contract. If it doesn't, no hard feelings. You decide, on your own timeline.

5

You Pick the Closing Date

Once you sign, you choose when we close. We complete the sale at a licensed local title company, the same way a traditional sale would.

Our Offer Isn't a Guess.

Every offer reflects a real look at your property, its condition, and today's market. It's not a random number, and we stand behind it.

Your Existing Mortgage or Liens Don't Stop a Sale.

At closing, the title company pays off your mortgage balance, any liens, and any back taxes directly from the sale proceeds before you receive the rest. You're not carrying that debt forward.

Good to know Every property's payoff situation is a little different. We'll walk through exactly what applies to yours before you sign anything, and your title company handles the details.

What We Guarantee, in Writing.

  • No fees or commissions, ever
  • No repairs, cleaning, or staging
  • No open houses, and a walkthrough only when the situation calls for one
  • You can walk away at any point before you sign
  • Your offer stands for a real window. We don't pressure you with expiring-tonight deadlines
